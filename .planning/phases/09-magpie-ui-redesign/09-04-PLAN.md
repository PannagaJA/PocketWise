## Objective
Harmonize all secondary and financial module screens to the Magpie visual standard: warm off-white canvas `#F8F9FE`, pure white cards with `rounded-3xl`, high-contrast typography, circular category containers, and sleek progress bars.

## Invariant Guarantees
- Keep all underlying calculation hooks, queries, mutations, and actions intact.
- Do not commit or stage any changes to Git.

## Tasks
1. Redesign `app/(tabs)/subscriptions.tsx`:
   - Canvas `#F8F9FE`.
   - Summary card with total monthly commitment in `text-[24px] font-extrabold text-zinc-950`.
   - Subscription cards: circular service icons (`44×44px`), frequency badge, renewal date countdown, and action menus.
2. Redesign `app/(tabs)/budgets.tsx`:
   - Canvas `#F8F9FE`.
   - Total budget overview card with slim progress track (`h-2 rounded-full bg-zinc-100`).
   - Category budget cards: pastel circular icon, spent vs limit typography, remaining amount, color-coded progress bars.
3. Redesign `app/goals.tsx`:
   - Canvas `#F8F9FE`.
   - Summary header for total saved.
   - Goal cards: target amount, saved amount, sleek progress bar, percentage badge, and `+ Add Money` pill button.
4. Redesign `app/bills.tsx`:
   - Canvas `#F8F9FE`.
   - Summary cards (Upcoming vs Paid).
   - Bill cards: amber calendar badge, due date countdown, amount, and `"Mark Paid"` button.
5. Redesign `app/lends.tsx`:
   - Canvas `#F8F9FE`.
   - Net balance header (You are owed vs You owe).
   - Person cards: avatar/initial circle, direction pill, amount, and settle trigger.
6. Redesign `app/reports.tsx`:
   - Canvas `#F8F9FE`.
   - Summary metric cards (Income, Expense, Net Savings) in white cards with subtle border.
   - Category spending breakdown bars and charts.
7. Redesign `app/(tabs)/more.tsx`:
   - Canvas `#F8F9FE`.
   - Grouped white surfaces with rounded corners, clean row icons, and chevrons for app settings.
