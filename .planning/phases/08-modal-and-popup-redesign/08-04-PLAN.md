## Objective
Migrate all 33+ modal instances across PocketWise to the new `AppBottomSheet`, `AppDialog`, and `AppLoadingIndicator` architecture while keeping 100% of business logic, form validation, and query invalidation intact.

2. Migrate `SmsOnboardingCard.tsx`, `SmsTransactionReviewModal.tsx`, `DatePickerModal.tsx`, and `TimePickerModal.tsx`.
3. Migrate `app/(tabs)/subscriptions.tsx` and `app/(tabs)/budgets.tsx` modals.
4. Migrate `app/bills.tsx`, `app/goals.tsx`, `app/lends.tsx`, and `app/(tabs)/more.tsx` modals.
5. Migrate Dashboard drawers in `app/(tabs)/index.tsx`.
