## Objective
Build the foundation of the Magpie-inspired modal design system: `AppLoadingIndicator`, `AppBottomSheet`, `AppDialog`, and `AppTextInput`.

## Tasks
1. Implement `AppLoadingIndicator.tsx` with signature alternating two-dot electric blue scaling & opacity pulse.
2. Implement `AppBottomSheet.tsx` with 28px rounded top corners, centered drag handle, standardized header, and keyboard-aware scroll container.
3. Implement `AppDialog.tsx` for centered confirmation and destructive decision popups.
4. Implement `AppTextInput.tsx` with auto-formatting for card number, expiry, CVV, and focus rings.
